# Governance

## Maintainers

Daniel Malmros (@danielmalmros) is the initial maintainer and owns decisions until more maintainers are added. People with write access are also listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).

Maintainers:

- review pull requests and issues
- accept or decline proposals
- publish releases
- enforce the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Decisions

A small change can go straight to a pull request. A large change, or a change to authentication, isolation, licensing, or this file, starts as a [proposal](docs/contributing/proposals.md).

An accepted proposal becomes a decision record in [docs/adr](docs/adr/README.md). A maintainer merges the record. Disagreement is settled by the maintainers, and the record states the reasons.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor who has landed several accepted changes, reviewed other people's work, and upheld the conduct and contribution rules can be nominated by a maintainer. Existing maintainers decide. A maintainer who is inactive for a long stretch, or who asks to step down, is removed the same way.

## Conduct reports

Report conduct concerns through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Impakta-io/inbai/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled under the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
